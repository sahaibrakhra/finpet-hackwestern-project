# FinPet — Financial Literacy & Wellness Companion

**Built for the Canada Life Track**

FinPet is an interactive personal finance companion that helps Gen Z build sustainable budgeting, saving, and investing habits by caring for a personalized virtual mascot. Instead of intimidating spreadsheets and financial jargon, your mascot acts as your primary interface—reacting to your real-world spending decisions, celebrating savings milestones, and translating complex financial concepts into daily, actionable habits.

---

## Why Gen Z Needs Financial Responsibility Now

Gen Z is entering adulthood amid rising living costs, student debt, and persistent inflation pressure. Recent Canadian financial insights highlight a critical gap between financial ambition and confidence:
* **High Motivation, High Anxiety:** Nearly one-third (32%) of Gen Z Canadians are actively motivated to invest more to secure their future, and 29% wish they had started investing earlier—leading all generations. Yet rising discretionary spending alongside economic uncertainty continues to fuel financial stress.
* **Redefining Security:** Half of Gen Z Canadians view building up to $100,000 in savings and investments as their benchmark for financial security, while 80% of Canadians identify the ability to cover an unexpected emergency expense as the foundation of peace of mind.
* **The Action Gap:** Young adults rarely lack the *desire* to manage money well; they lack an approachable, low-friction system that turns abstract long-term goals (like emergency funds, TFSAs, and compound growth) into immediate, rewarding feedback loops.
